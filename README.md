# MUNMeet
MUN organisation + participation app that you deserve!

## Proposed Control Flow

### Delegate
Pre-event:
- Joins the app
- Creates an account (Joins a school if is a part of it)
[delegate]
- Chooses an event to participate in
- Completes a form & awaits for approval
- If rejected: try again?
if accepted: 
- gets a form for position paper and calendar events and rest are assigned to the user
- gets linked to a thread for students of the same committee/joins a group chat?
Mid-event:
- Can see a timetable of the event + locations
- Can collaborate on working documents
- Can vote
Post-event:
- Gets a digital copy of certificate/award/badge
- Gets personal analytics
- Can get access to media
[delegate]

### School
Pre-event:
- Joins the app
- Creates an school account and links all the students to it
[superviser]
- Chooses list of students and an event to participate in
Mid-event:
- Share announcements to own students
- Can see a timetable of the event + locations of all their students
- Involved with real-time communication feed + alerted in case of emergencies
Post-event:
- Gets a digital copies of certificate/award/badge + results + analytics of each of the students
- Can get access to media
[superviser]

### Organiser:
Pre-event:
- Joins the app
[organiser]
Pre-planning:
- registers an organization
- can invite other people with different levels of access to help out: chairs, volunteers, and more.
organisation:
During-planning:
- create general conference information (for autogenerating website): dates, location, committees, timetable/schedule, age age limits, experience levels, deadlines...
during the event
- approve/reject applications and accept financing, collect money
- review position papers
During-event:
- Share announcements to specific groups
- Control the flow of the debate + all the mymun things
- Chairs record notes on the delegates
- Decide on the awards given out and time of announcement + time of publishing
Post-event
- Release the photo album sets
- Feedback form release# ContestClerk
