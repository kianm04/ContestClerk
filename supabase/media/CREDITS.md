# Where the sample photographs came from

Every photograph in `public-media/conferences/` (T149). Each one was taken from Unsplash
through its import on Wikimedia Commons, where it is published under the
[CC0 1.0 public domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to
use commercially, with no permission or credit required. The credit is written down here
anyway, so anybody can check the source and the licence of a file before relying on it.

The photographs were cropped to 1600×600 and saved as JPEG at about 200 KB. Paths are in the
`public-media` bucket, with the middle of each id shortened to `…`.

`client/static/hero.jpg` is not one of them. It is a pale assembly hall the product owner
supplied on the Trello card
[cI0TyDmi](https://trello.com/c/cI0TyDmi/21-a-new-hero-banner-img) on 1 Oct 2026, scaled to
1600×900 and saved as JPEG at about 290 KB. It replaced T149's photograph of Oxford and stood
behind the landing page hero until Trello RBz46aTQ, then behind the `/home` greeting band
until the home page was rebuilt around the reader's next conference (`docs/home.md`) on
30 Oct 2026. Nothing shows it now; it is kept on disk because the owner chose it.

Nor are the two pictures behind the landing page hero, `client/static/landing-delegates.webp`
(a Gothic university with cherry blossom, also the sample conference's banner under the hero)
and `client/static/landing-organisers.webp` (a sunlit desk with a laptop). The product owner
supplied both on the Trello card
[RBz46aTQ](https://trello.com/c/RBz46aTQ/27-rework-the-non-logged-in-landing-page) on
1 Oct 2026. They were scaled to 1672×941, the size of that card's mockup, and saved as WebP at
about 130 KB and 55 KB.

## The logos

Every logo in `public-media/organizations/` and every `logo-b1000000-….png` in
`public-media/conferences/` is an invented crest: a shield, a simple pattern and the
initials of the organisation or conference. None is a real school's or society's mark, and
none is the United Nations emblem. They were drawn for this repository, so there is nobody
to credit and no licence to follow. Their sources are the SVG files in `../drawn/`, one per
crest, named after the organisation's or conference's slug. The bucket accepts only JPEG,
PNG and WebP, so each was rendered to a 512×512 PNG with
`rsvg-convert -w 512 -h 512 <slug>.svg -o logo.png`. To change a crest, edit its SVG, render
it again and replace the PNG at the same path.

The five Northbridge conferences share the Northbridge MUN Society crest and the London
photograph. Each still has its own copy, because a picture is stored under the conference
it belongs to.

`client/static/committee-emblem.svg`, the emblem beside every committee on `/home`, was
drawn the same way: a plain globe in a round seal. It is not the United Nations emblem,
whose use General Assembly resolution 92(I) keeps for the United Nations itself.
